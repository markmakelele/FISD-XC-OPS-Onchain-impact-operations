Method and limitations
The score is triage guidance, not a guarantee.

Each score combines recency, native-value magnitude relative to the loaded sample, interaction type, observed outcome and direction. Recommendations are rules-based. A high score means “inspect sooner,” not “fraud.” Verify every live claim against the linked Explorer record.
