Turn onchain activity into a next best action.

Monitor a wallet or contract, interpret recent activity through an explainable signal model, then trace every signal back to Blockscout Explorer evidence. FISD XC adds an operational layer above the explorer: what changed, when it changed, and what to inspect next.

Product hypothesisExplorer data is the evidence layer. FISD XC is the decision layer. Every signal below remains linked to its source transaction; scores are triage heuristics, not financial valuation.
